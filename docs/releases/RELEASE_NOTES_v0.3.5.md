## Fortnite Visual Chest Counter v0.3.5

- Dashboard shows match phase, replay verification, team sync and capture health. Setup checks the selected source, HUD, Fortnite log, replay reader and Group configuration.
- Recover an interrupted match and verify it using the Fortnite match ID. Saved partial results stay local until verified.
- Discord retries pending replay corrections across restarts under the original contribution ID. Match history shows delivery status and can retry a selected match.
- Stats filters matches by detection quality and capture configuration. Its comparison uses original live chest types, even after replay verification.
- Export a short missed-chest diagnostic ZIP from Capture health.
